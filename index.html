Why FeedMe

Built like a well-worn notebook, not a spreadsheet

Every spot you add becomes part of a shared table other people can pull up a chair to.

🌶️

Rate it your way

Spice gets chilis, staff get smileys, atmosphere gets stars, and the overall verdict gets a thumbs up — fixed icons that actually mean something at a glance.

🥗

Dietary heads-up

Save your restrictions once. FeedMe warns you when a spot doesn't list an option that matches — vegan, halal, gluten-free, and more.

🌍

18 languages, one map

From Swedish to Vietnamese to Arabic — the whole app, not just a translated menu bar.

A living map

Double-tap to drop a pin. That's it.

No forms to dread. Add the basics, snap a photo of the food and the place, and rate what mattered — service, atmosphere, cleanliness, or just whether you'd go back.
